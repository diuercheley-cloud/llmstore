# Support & Maintenance Terms

## 1. Scope
These terms govern the support services provided for the LLM Inference Stack.

## 2. Response Times (SLA)
| Priority | Response Time | Resolution Goal |
|---|---|---|
| P1 (Critical) | 1 Hour | 24 Hours |
| P2 (High) | 4 Hours | 3 Business Days |
| P3 (Normal) | 1 Business Day | Next Release |

## 3. Exclusions
- Hardware failures.
- Third-party software issues.
- Unauthorized modifications to the software.

## 4. Support Hours
- Standard: 9 AM - 5 PM (Customer Local Time)
- Emergency: 24/7/365
